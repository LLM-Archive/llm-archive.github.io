# Notice

## What this is

LLM-Archive is an independent **research project**. It keeps an open, dated, checkable record of one
narrow property: whether a model keeps the same decision when a question is reworded without changing
its meaning. The formal statement of purpose is in [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md) §1.2.

## Disclaimer

Results are specific to one frozen panel of scenarios, one model and one date. They are provided **as
is, without warranty**, and are not advice or a basis for deciding whether to buy, use or trust any
model. The project is not a product evaluation, a ranking or a certification, and it is not run to
rate, endorse or promote any provider or model.

LLM-Archive is **not affiliated with, and is not endorsed by, any model provider**. The names of
models, products and companies are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify what was
measured.

## What is sent to models, and what is published

The scenarios sent to models are fictional decision dilemmas. No personal data is sent to a model or
collected from one. Raw model responses are published word for word only for the protocols marked
`open` (`open-lane/<year>.jsonl`); for every other protocol only outcome categories or counts are
published.

## Privacy of this site

The site loads no third-party scripts, stylesheets, fonts or images, and sets no cookies. The only
thing it stores in your browser is your choice of theme (`localStorage`, on your own device, never
sent anywhere). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; any access logging by that host is the host's own.
